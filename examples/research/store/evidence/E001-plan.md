id: E001
title: Synthetic experiment plan
---

This is invented teaching data, not a scientific result. The plan is declared
first in the replay; its date alone would not prove independent preregistration.

Before interpreting a comparison, verify a known positive and delivered dose against this plan.
Before closing the study, record one replication against the same criteria.

Criteria: the known positive must measure at least 5 units, delivered dose must
be 9 to 11 units, and the absolute treatment-minus-control difference must be
at least 1 unit to meet the effect criterion. A smaller difference supports
only "the declared effect criterion was not met in this comparison". It does
not establish no effect in other conditions or populations.

After an interruption, retain the partial observations, describe any deviation,
and restart the comparison with a fresh run id. Do not pool partial and fresh
observations. Record controls and dose for the fresh run before interpretation.
